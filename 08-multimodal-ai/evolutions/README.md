# Multimodal AI - Evolutions & Future Directions

Emerging research and future directions in multimodal AI.

## Current Frontiers (2024-2025)

### World Models
- Understanding physical dynamics from video
- Predicting future frames
- Learning physics from observation
- Sora, Runway Gen-3

### Unified Multimodal Models
- Single model for all modalities
- Gemini, GPT-4o approach
- Any-to-any generation
- Challenges in training and architecture

### Controllable Generation
- ControlNet for fine-grained control
- IP-Adapter for style transfer
- Consistent characters across images
- Video generation with temporal consistency

### Real-Time Multimodal
- Voice mode (GPT-4o)
- Streaming vision processing
- Low-latency generation
- Edge deployment

## Open Research Questions

1. **Video understanding:** Can we truly understand long videos?
2. **Physical reasoning:** Can models learn intuitive physics?
3. **Generation quality:** Closing the gap to human perception
4. **Consistency:** Maintaining identity across generations
5. **Efficiency:** Making multimodal models practical

## Emerging Modalities

- 3D understanding and generation
- Tactile/haptic feedback
- Robotics integration
- Scientific data (molecules, proteins, etc.)

## Speculative Futures (2026+)

- Real-time video generation
- Embodied AI with multimodal sensing
- Creative AI that truly "imagines"
- Seamless human-AI multimodal communication
