# Emerging AI Trends - References

## Reasoning & Test-Time Compute

### System 1 / System 2
- **Test-Time Compute: From System-1 to System-2 Thinking** (Ji et al., 2025)
  - Comprehensive survey of test-time compute methods
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02497

- **Thinking, Fast and Slow** — Daniel Kahneman
  - The psychological foundation for dual-process theory

### Chain-of-Thought
- **Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models** (Wei et al., 2022)
  - The foundational CoT paper
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903

- **Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with LLMs** (Yao et al., 2023)
  - Extending CoT to tree search
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601

- **Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought Reasoning** (Wang et al., 2022)
  - Sampling multiple reasoning paths
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171

### Looped Transformers
- **Universal Transformers** (Dehghani et al., 2018)
  - Early work on adaptive computation
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.03819

- **Looped Transformers for Length Generalization** (2025-2026)
  - Modern looped architectures for reasoning
