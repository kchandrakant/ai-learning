# AI Ethics & Responsible AI: Beyond the Basics

Emerging trends and future directions in AI ethics.

## Current Frontiers (2025-2026)

### Scalable Oversight
As AI systems become more capable:
- AI-assisted evaluation of AI outputs
- Debate and amplification approaches
- Recursive reward modeling

### Mechanistic Alignment
Moving from behavioral to mechanistic understanding:
- Interpretability research (Anthropic, DeepMind)
- Feature circuits and sparse autoencoders
- Understanding what models actually represent

### Governance at Scale
As AI deployment accelerates:
- Automated compliance checking
- Real-time bias monitoring
- AI auditing industry emergence

## Open Research Questions

### 1. Evaluation Problem
How do we evaluate alignment for systems smarter than us?
- Current: Human evaluators
- Future: AI-assisted, but trust bootstrapping problem

### 2. Value Pluralism
Whose values should AI systems encode?
- Different cultures, contexts, individuals
- Can we build systems that adapt?

### 3. Long-Term Alignment
Beyond current deployments:
- How do we ensure AI remains aligned over time?
- Self-improvement risks
- Value drift

### 4. Liability and Accountability
As AI makes more decisions:
- Who is responsible when AI causes harm?
- Insurance and liability frameworks
- Legal personhood questions
