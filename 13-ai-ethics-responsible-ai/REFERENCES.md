# AI Ethics & Responsible AI - References

## Foundational Papers

### AI Safety & Alignment
- **Concrete Problems in AI Safety** (Amodei et al., 2016)
  - Defines key safety problems: safe exploration, distributional shift, reward hacking
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565

- **Risks from Learned Optimization** (Hubinger et al., 2019)
  - Inner alignment and mesa-optimization
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01820

- **Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback** (Bai et al., 2022)
  - Self-improvement through constitutional principles
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073

### Bias & Fairness
- **Gender Shades** (Buolamwini & Gebru, 2018)
  - Intersectional accuracy disparities in commercial facial recognition
  - https://proceedings.mlr.press/v81/buolamwini18a.html

- **On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots** (Bender et al., 2021)
  - Risks of large language models
  - https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3442188.3445922

- **Fairness and Machine Learning** (Barocas, Hardt, Narayanan)
  - Comprehensive textbook on fairness
  - https://fairmlbook.org/

### Interpretability
- **Attention is not Explanation** (Jain & Wallace, 2019)
  - Limitations of attention-based explanations
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.10186

- **A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions** (SHAP, Lundberg & Lee, 2017)
  - Shapley values for feature attribution
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07874

- **Towards Monosemanticity** (Anthropic, 2023)
  - Mechanistic interpretability with sparse autoencoders
  - https://transformer-circuits.pub/2023/monosemantic-features

### Documentation & Transparency
- **Model Cards for Model Reporting** (Mitchell et al., 2019)
  - Standardized model documentation
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993

- **Datasheets for Datasets** (Gebru et al., 2021)
  - Standardized dataset documentation
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010

## Regulatory Frameworks

### EU AI Act
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689** — Artificial Intelligence Act
- Risk-based approach: unacceptable, high, limited, minimal risk
- Requirements for high-risk AI systems
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689

### NIST AI RMF
- **AI Risk Management Framework 1.0** (2023)
- Four core functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage
- https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

### US Executive Order
- **Executive Order on Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy AI** (Oct 2023)
- Requirements for dual-use foundation models
- https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefing-room/presidential-actions/2023/10/30/executive-order-on-the-safe-secure-and-trustworthy-development-and-use-of-artificial-intelligence/
