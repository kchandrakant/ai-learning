## 🗓️ Weekly Reading System

### My Schedule Template

```
MONDAY (30 min)
├── Skim newsletter digests
├── Add interesting papers to reading list
└── Check Papers With Code for new SOTAs

WEDNESDAY (1 hour)
├── Pass 1-2 on 2-3 papers from list
├── Deep read (Pass 3) on 1 important paper
└── Write brief notes/summary

FRIDAY (30 min)
├── Browse Twitter/Reddit for discussions
├── Watch 1 Yannic Kilcher video
└── Update paper database

ONGOING
├── Bookmark interesting threads
├── Note concepts to learn later
└── Implement something from a paper (monthly)
```

### Reading Priorities

```
MUST READ (Foundation)
├── Papers cited in courses you're taking
├── Papers that introduce methods you use
└── Best paper awards from top conferences

SHOULD READ (Staying current)
├── Papers solving problems in your area
├── New SOTA on tasks you care about
└── Papers recommended by trusted sources

NICE TO READ (Exploration)
├── Adjacent areas for inspiration
├── Survey/review papers
└── Papers that seem interesting
```

## ⚠️ Avoiding Information Overload

### Signs You're Overloaded

- [ ] Hundreds of unread tabs/bookmarks
- [ ] FOMO about every new paper
- [ ] Reading widely but not deeply
- [ ] Knowing names but not understanding methods

### Strategies

1. **Set limits**
   - Max 30 min/day on "staying current"
   - Max 2-3 newsletters
   - Unsubscribe aggressively

2. **Batch processing**
   - Designated times for paper reading
   - No context-switching throughout day

3. **Depth over breadth**
   - Better to deeply understand 5 papers than skim 50
   - Implementation teaches more than reading

4. **Just-in-time learning**
   - Learn things when you need them
   - Not everything requires immediate attention

5. **Accept missing things**
   - You WILL miss papers — that's okay
   - Important ideas resurface
